# Prison Escape: a procedural 3D short

A fully procedural, ~17-second cinematic shot in Blender (Cycles). A bearded
prisoner in a khaki uniform runs across a prison yard, climbs the chain-link
perimeter fence, perches on the top rail, leaps down, and sprints away from the
cell block. It's modelled on four reference frames: a low camera behind the
fence, the climb over the bar, the low-angle jump, and the run toward the camera.

**Output:** [`output/prison_escape.mp4`](output/prison_escape.mp4) (1920×804, 2.39:1, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC)

Everything is generated by code: the character's shape, rig, clothing and hair,
the environment, the animation, the six camera shots and the soundtrack. There
is no hand-keyed or downloaded animation.

## Shots

| # | Shot | What happens |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | Long-lens tracking | He runs across the yard; the cell block fills the frame |
| 2 | Through the fence | Low dolly outside the chain-link, tracking him in profile (reference 1) |
| 3 | The climb | Steps on the plinth, jump-grabs the mesh, climbs hand over hand |
| 4 | Over the top | Close-up as he mantles the top rail and climbs onto it (reference 2) |
| 5 | The drop | Low angle as he leaps off with arms spread and lands (reference 3) |
| 6 | The sprint | Camera vehicle backs away as he sprints toward it, then he runs past (reference 4) |

## How it works

- `escape/character.py`: builds the man from the CC0 **MakeHuman** base mesh
  (via MPFB2 data). It applies shape targets for an adult male, builds the
  game-engine skeleton from the joint cubes, and adds skin weights. The khaki
  shirt (with collar, pockets and placket), the straight-leg trousers, the
  sneakers, the particle hair, the beard and the brows are all generated from
  the body surface.
- `escape/rigmath.py`: a small pose solver. Controllers (pelvis, chest, head,
  hand and foot targets, knee and elbow poles, finger curl and toe bend) are
  solved with analytic two-bone IK and baked to per-frame quaternion keys.
- `escape/locomotion.py`: procedural running. It plans footsteps from the
  cadence and stance time, locks the feet with heel-to-toe roll, and adds
  heel-kick swing arcs, pelvis bob and rotation, counter-rotating shoulders,
  and arm pumping driven by sagittal FK.
- `escape/choreo.py`: the performance. It runs the yard path, then uses
  monotone-Hermite keyframe tracks for the climb, mantle, perch on the rail,
  ballistic leap and landing. Last, it blends into an accelerating sprint.
  It also logs contact events (steps, grabs, clanks, landing) for the sound.
- `escape/environment.py`: the procedural environment:
  - a cell block with recessed barred windows and pilasters, and an
    administration wing
  - a guard tower, light poles and jersey barriers
  - the fence: a concrete plinth, posts, rails, and a shader-based chain-link
    mesh
  - about 2 million grass blades as static hair curves
  - geometry-node trees and tree lines
  - a physical sky with a procedural cloud layer
- `escape/cameras.py`: six keyframed cameras with depth of field, focus
  tracking and handheld shake, switched by timeline markers.
- `scripts/make_audio.py`: synthesizes the soundtrack from the contact events:
  - footsteps on grass, dirt and concrete
  - chain-link rattles and pipe clanks
  - a whoosh and the landing thud
  - breaths
  - a distant two-tone prison siren
  - wind
  - a tension score of drone, pulses and braams
- `scripts/encode.sh`: colour grade, vignette and film grain, fades, an
  upscale to 1920×804, and muxing.

## Rebuild

```bash
uv venv --python python3.13 .venv && VIRTUAL_ENV=.venv uv pip install bpy==5.2.2 numpy
.venv/bin/python scripts/build_scene.py --out build/escape.blend          # scene + animation + cameras
.venv/bin/python scripts/render.py --blend build/escape.blend --out frames  # Cycles, resumable
.venv/bin/python scripts/make_audio.py --events build/escape_events.json --out build/audio.wav
scripts/encode.sh frames build/audio.wav output/prison_escape.mp4
```

Useful previews:
- `scripts/build_scene.py --lite` drops the grass and trees.
- `scripts/render.py --preset animatic` renders at low resolution.
- `scripts/test_character.py`, `scripts/test_run.py` and
  `scripts/preview_anim.py` are look-development helpers.
